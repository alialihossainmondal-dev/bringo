Shipping and delivery

Last updated 7 November 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses in India. If we cannot deliver to your address, we will contact you before dispatching your order.

How long it takes

  • Orders are packed and handed to our delivery partner in about 1 to 2 working days.
  • Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days from the day you place the order.
  • Remote areas, public holidays and weather can add time. If your order is delayed, we will let you know.

Delivery charges

Any delivery charge is shown at checkout before you place your order. The total you see is the total you pay.

Tracking your order

Open Orders to see the status of each order: Placed, Packing, Shipped and Delivered. For courier tracking details, message us on WhatsApp with your order number.

Cash on delivery

For cash on delivery orders, please keep the exact amount ready when the parcel arrives. If a cash on delivery parcel is refused without a valid reason, we may ask for prepaid payment on future orders.

Prepaid orders

Prepaid orders are confirmed with a one-time Master Code that matches your order amount. Your order is processed once the code is accepted at checkout.

Before you sign for a parcel

Check that the outer packaging is intact. If it looks damaged or opened, tell the delivery person and message us straight away with a photo. See our returns policy.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp or visit the contact page.
