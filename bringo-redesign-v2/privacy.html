Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy explains how Bringo handles your personal information when you use our website and place orders.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account when you log in.
  • Order details: your mobile number, delivery address, order notes, the items you order, coupon and Master Code used, and your order history.
  • Cart: items in your cart are stored in your own browser until you check out.
  • Messages: anything you send us on WhatsApp or Instagram.

How we use it

  • To take, pack, deliver and support your orders.
  • To verify prepaid payments and prevent fraud or misuse of coupons and codes.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns and refunds.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners receive your name, mobile number and address so they can deliver your order.
  • Google and Firebase provide our login and database. Your account and order data is stored with them.
  • Hosting and image services that serve our website and product images may receive technical data such as your IP address.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to run your order, handle returns and meet accounting and legal requirements. You can ask us to delete your account details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by messaging us. You can update your saved number and address any time from your account.

Security

We use trusted services to store your data and limit who can see it. No online system is completely secure, so please keep your Google account protected.

Children

Bringo is not meant for children under 18. Please ask a parent or guardian to place orders.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any privacy question, message us on WhatsApp or use the contact page.
