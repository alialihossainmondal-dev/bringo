Returns and refunds

Last updated 7 November 2026

When you can return an item

You can ask for a return, replacement or refund within 7 days of delivery if:

  • the item is damaged, defective or not working,
  • you received the wrong item, size or colour, or
  • an item is missing from your order.

What cannot be returned

  • Opened food, personal care and hygiene items, for safety reasons, unless they arrive damaged or wrong.
  • Items that have been used, washed or damaged after delivery.
  • Items returned without their original packaging or accessories.

How to ask for a return

  1. Message usContact us on WhatsApp within the return window. Share your order number.
  2. Send a photoA clear photo or short video of the item and its packaging helps us resolve things faster.
  3. We confirmWe will tell you whether to send the item back, or arrange a replacement or refund directly.

Refunds

Once your return is approved and the item is checked, we refund the amount you paid for it. Prepaid orders are refunded to the account you paid from. For cash on delivery orders we refund by UPI or bank transfer to details you share with us. We will tell you the expected refund time when we approve your return.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order before it ships. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you can still return eligible items under this policy.
