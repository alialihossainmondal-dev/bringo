About Bringo

Bringo is an online store for everyday essentials. You pick what you need, tell us where to send it, and we deliver it to your door.

How an order works

  1. Choose your itemsBrowse by category or search, then add what you need to your cart.
  2. Check out in a minuteLog in with Google, add your mobile number and address, and pick cash on delivery or prepaid UPI.
  3. We pack and ship itYour order moves from placed, to packing, to shipped, to delivered.
  4. Follow it, and ask us anythingOpen the Orders page for live status, or message us on WhatsApp.

Prepaid payments, kept safe

For prepaid orders we issue a one-time Master Code that is tied to your exact order amount. It can be used once, for that amount only, so a payment cannot be reused or applied to a different order.

Talk to a real person

Questions before you buy, or a problem after your order arrives? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help. You can also read our shipping and returns policies at any time.
