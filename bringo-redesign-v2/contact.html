Common questions

How do I place an order?

Add items to your cart, open the cart and tap Go to checkout. Log in with Google, enter your mobile number and full address, choose how you want to pay, and place the order.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes, on most items. Products marked Prepaid only must be paid in advance by UPI. Checkout shows which options are available for the items in your cart.

How does prepaid payment work?

At checkout choose Prepaid and tap Get Master Code on WhatsApp. We reply with payment details. After you pay the exact total, we send a one-time Master Code. Enter it at checkout to confirm your order. The code only works once, for that amount.

How do I track my order?

Open Orders from the menu. Each order shows its status: Placed, Packing, Shipped or Delivered. For anything else, message us with your order number.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, before it ships. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number as soon as you can. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can still return eligible items under our returns policy.

My item arrived damaged or wrong. What now?

Message us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo. We will arrange a replacement or refund. See the returns policy for details.
